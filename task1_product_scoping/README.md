# Task 1 - Product Scoping

## Objective

To design an internal tool that helps marketing teams understand performance across channels.

## Proposed Solution

I designed a Marketing Performance Dashboard Assistant.

The dashboard helps users:

- View marketing performance
- Compare channels
- Analyse ROI
- Receive recommendations

## Why This Approach

I focused on simplicity and usability.

A focused solution is more valuable than an overcomplicated one.

## Included in V1

- Dashboard
- Channel comparison
- Recommendations

## Excluded from V1

- AI chatbot
- Forecasting
- Real-time alerts

## Future Improvements

Future versions may include AI insights and predictive analytics.
